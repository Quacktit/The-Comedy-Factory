Our Story

Since 2011

The Comedy Factory is a trailblazing entertainment venture — the first of its kind in India. Conceptualized by Manan Desai, Co-Founder & Programming Director, who spent seven years hosting prime-time shows on FM radio stations across Gujarat.

Driven by a hunger for live entertainment in the state, Manan founded TCF to deliver original content and real humor to audiences who deserved better. Today, we have taken our performances way beyond Gujarat — to the UK, Canada, USA, Australia, and beyond.

We pioneered Urban Standup Comedy in Gujarat and have since expanded into Improv, Musical, and Sketch Comedy. Popular names like Zakir Khan, Vir Das, Kenny Sebastian, and Kanan Gill have brought their shows to Gujarat through us.
