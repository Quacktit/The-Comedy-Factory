Who We Are

Gujarat's Comedy Pioneer

The Comedy Factory is a trailblazing entertainment venture — the first of its kind in India. It was conceptualized by Manan Desai, Co-Founder & Programming Director, who spent over seven years hosting prime-time shows on leading FM radio stations across Gujarat.

Driven by a hunger for live entertainment in the state, he founded The Comedy Factory in 2011 to provide audiences with original content and humor. What started as a bold experiment in a state better known for its business culture quickly became Gujarat's most beloved entertainment institution.

With time, our performances took us way beyond Gujarat — including corporate affairs and public shows in countries like the UK, Canada, USA, and Australia. We are proud to have pioneered Urban Standup Comedy in Gujarat.

Popular comedic voices of India including Zakir Khan, Amit Tandon, Vir Das, Kanan Gill, Abishek Upmanyu, and Kenny Sebastian have brought their live shows to Gujarat through us.

Our Journey

15 Years in Numbers

2011

The Comedy Factory is Born

Manan Desai launches TCF in Ahmedabad, performing the first live standup comedy show in Gujarat. The audience of 50 becomes the seed of a movement.

2013

Expanding Across Gujarat

TCF begins performing across Surat, Vadodara, and Rajkot. Improv and Musical Comedy formats are introduced. Audiences double with every show.

2015

Gujarati Cinema Debut

TCF artists make their Gujarati cinema debut. Chirayu Mistry joins the ensemble. First corporate show for a Fortune 500 company in Ahmedabad.

2018

First International Tour

TCF takes its first international tour to the UK and Canada. Gujarati comedy lands on international stages for the very first time.

2021

10 Years & Going Strong

TCF celebrates its 10th anniversary with a sold-out mega show in Ahmedabad. 300+ shows completed. YouTube channel crosses 1 million views.

2026

The Story Continues

500+ shows. 8 countries. 50,000+ audience members. And this is only the beginning. The Comedy Factory is poised to soar even higher.
