What time should I arrive? +
Doors open 30 minutes before the show starts. We recommend arriving early to grab the best seats and buy snacks!
Is there an age restriction? +
Most of our standup shows are 16+. Please check the specific event details on the booking page before purchasing.
Are tickets refundable? +
Tickets are non-refundable unless the show is cancelled or rescheduled by The Comedy Factory.
Do you offer corporate bookings? +
Yes! We perform at corporate events, college fests, and private parties. Check out our Contact page to send an enquiry.
