The Faces of TCF

Each member brings a unique voice, style, and energy to the stage. Together, we are The Comedy Factory.

Founder & Director

Manan Desai

Co-Founder & Programming Director

Radio Jockey turned comedy visionary. Manan founded TCF in 2011 after 7 years on Gujarat's top FM stations. A performer, producer, and writer, he has taken Gujarat's comedy global — performing in the UK, Canada, USA, and Australia. He acts. He produces. He performs. Mostly for his wife.
