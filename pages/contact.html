Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
For corporate and private events, we recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks in advance to allow time for custom material. For public shows, tickets are available until sold out.
Do you perform outside Gujarat?
Absolutely! We've performed across India and in 8 countries internationally. Travel and accommodation for outstation shows can be arranged.
Can the content be customised for our company?
Yes — and we love doing it. We write custom material based on your company, industry, team culture, and inside jokes. It always gets the biggest laughs.
What languages do you perform in?
We perform in Gujarati, Hindi, and English — or any combination. Just let us know your audience's preference when you enquire.
What is the minimum show duration?
Our shortest corporate sets are 20 minutes. Full-length shows run between 60–90 minutes. We can tailor the duration to your event's schedule.
How do ticket refunds work?
Tickets are non-refundable unless a show is cancelled by TCF. In that case, a full refund is processed within 5–7 business days via the original payment method.
